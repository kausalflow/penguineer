Results on Homeostat¶

How well penguineer 0.2.0 does on plants whose truth is known. Every number on this page comes from Homeostat plants: simulated, with the cause of each off-spec lot injected and recorded, so that the answer can be checked. Real plants are messier, so treat these numbers as an upper bound for your plant, not a promise.

Root causes: the bench¶

bench homeostat builds plants of one kind (two feeds, a mixer, a heater, a reactor with a temperature loop and a lab analysis of the product), injects one of eight faults into each, and runs investigate on the first off-spec lot exactly as on a real plant. Three splits use different seeds:

  • train: the test weights and rule scores in calibration.json were fitted on it;
  • validation: the confidence thresholds were chosen on it, and every change was checked against it;
  • hidden: its seed is a repository secret. It was run for this release, by the Release gate workflow, which prints only the scores below.
Split Scenarios hit@1 hit@3 Wrong-confident Data-fault catch
validation 85 89% 96% 5% 95% (19 of 20)
hidden 171 87% 93% (88% to 96%) 5% (3% to 10%) 95% (85% to 99%, n=44)
gate ≥ 150 ≥ 70% < 10% ≥ 90%

The hidden split was generated from 200 plants (25 per fault type); 29 never made an off-spec lot and were skipped. A first run on 160 plants scored the same within a point (hit@3 93%, wrong-confident 5%, data-fault catch 94%) but had only 136 scenarios, under the gate's 150, so the default grew to 200. The intervals are 95% Wilson intervals. The hidden scores sit a few points under validation's, as expected for a split nothing was tuned on.

On validation, by fault type:

Fault n hit@1 hit@3
catalyst_loss 12 92% 100%
feed_b_flow 10 80% 100%
feed_flow 7 100% 100%
feed_property 12 83% 100%
reactor_fouling 12 83% 83%
reactor_sensor_bias 12 92% 92%
reactor_sensor_drift 8 100% 100%
reactor_setpoint 12 92% 100%

Fouling is the hardest: it builds slowly, so by the first off-spec lot it looks much like a change in the reactor's own behaviour. The one data fault missed on validation is a small sensor bias that started inside the newest reference lot; see Limits.

Delays: lag¶

lag was checked on six bench plants whose volumes and back-mixing are known, fitting feed B's flow against the lab's product property. With an hourly lab result over 20 days, the dead-plus-mixing model finds the mean delay within 1.7 minutes on average (the fixed dead time is off by 3.5 minutes, and by 14 on the plant with the most mixing). With a result every 4 hours over 60 days, within 2.1 minutes.

What these numbers do not say¶

  • All plants are of one kind. A distillation column, a batch reactor or a plant with recycle has not been benchmarked.
  • One fault per plant. Two causes at once are not in the bench.
  • The plant graph is the true one. With a drafted graph (graph draft) an edge that is missing hides its cause; the confirm queue in graph check is there for that.
  • The LLM is not scored: the bench runs without it, and the LLM never changes the ranking.
